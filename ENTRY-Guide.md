### Scholarships Paperwork

#### MoE Scholarship
Scholarship program by MoE Taiwan for international students who wants to study at Taiwan
- open registration at 1 February - 31 March (2025 Cycle).
- Master/Doctoral Degree = 20,000 NTD per month
- Subsidy for tuition/misc up to NT$ 40,000 per semester.
- Scholarship duration =  2 years

Required Documents:
1. Fill out the Application Form
2. Gather passport, Kartu Keluarga, photo, etc.
3. Prepare diploma + transcript (bachelor if applying for master’s). If not in English/Chinese, translate.
4. Register/apply to the Taiwanese university you want. Because being selected for the scholarship still requires you to get into a university.
5. Obtain two recommendation letters (signed/sealed).
6. Language certification:
    - If program taught in English: proof of English proficiency (TOEFL etc).
    - If program taught not in English: TOCFL required.
7. Legalise documents up to the step required by the Taiwanese rep office in TETO

Source = [Guidelines](https://taiwanscholarship.moe.gov.tw/web/pages.aspx?p=7), [Procedures](https://www.uksw.edu/ministry-of-education-taiwan-scholarship-tahun-2024/)

#### Asus Scholarship
ASUS and Taiwan Tech launched a new scholarship program for international students to pursue their education and future careers in Taiwan. 
- For International Student
- Fields: Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Management, Design, or closely related fields.
- **[IMPORTANT]** Applicants should not already receive other forms of financial aid from NTUST or from Taiwan government, and should not be concurrently enrolled in another university or full-time employed in Taiwan.
- Tuition fee waiver for up to two years of study.
- Living allowance: NT$ 12,000 per month for a master’s student for up to 2 years.
- After graduation, scholarship recipients may be offered a 2-year position at ASUS.
- **[IMPORTANT]** Requires Chinese language proficiency at the TOCFL A2 level, with the ability to communicate effectively in daily life situations.
- Application deadline: August 15, 2022 - September 16, 2022 5 PM (GST+8)

##### **Required documents:**
- Bachelor’s degree certificate + transcripts
- CV/Resume
- Statement of purpose / study plan
- Recommendation letters (likely 2)
- Proof of Chinese proficiency (TOCFL A2 or equivalent) or check if program allows English-taught track and whether language requirement is waived.
- Keep your passport, identification ready.

#### CTCI Scholarship
The CTCI Foundation offers scholarships for overseas graduate students in science, engineering, or related fields at Taiwanese universities. </br>
There are two types:
1. Research Scholarship for Overseas Students — for outstanding students.
2. Bursary Award for Overseas Students — for students with economic difficulties.

Example amounts: Research Scholarship ~ NT$ 150,000 per recipient. Bursary ~ NT$ 100,000.

##### **Eligibility & Requirements**:
- Must be enrolled in a Master’s or PhD programme in Taiwan in a designated department/institute/university in science & engineering or related fields.
- The student status must be current (i.e., you are already enrolled in Taiwan) for the CTCI case.
- Each department is allowed to recommend at most one candidate in many cases.
- Academic performance criteria (e.g., average score above certain threshold): For example, the Chemical Engineering department at NTUST says for the Research Scholarship: average academic score above 80 points; for the bursary: above 75 points.
- If you apply via your department you submit your documents via your university to CTCI Foundation
- Application period (for 2025 cycle): July 1 – September 10, 2025 (postmark deadline).
- Some universities indicate a local deadline (to submit to department/unit) by July 31 for hard copy docs.

##### **How to Apply**:
- You must be a candidate recommended by your department/institute.
- Your department collects your documents (CV, transcript, student ID copy, research abstracts, recommendation letter, etc). Example list:
  - Student ID card copy.
  - Original transcripts of all semesters (Chinese or English)
  - Autobiography (Chinese or English)
  - Recommendation letter from advisor.
  - Research abstract and preliminary results.
  - For bursary award: financial outline of expenses/income.

Submit online registration (starting July 1) and then submit hard-copies to your department by the internal deadline and department forwards to CTCI Foundation.


#### AAEON Scholarship
The AAEON Scholarship (sometimes under “研揚獎學金” at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology or similar universities) is offered to graduate students in certain departments (e.g., Electronic & Computer Engineering, Electro-Optical Engineering) at NTUST (Taiwan Tech).

##### **Eligibility & Requirements**
- Full-time Master’s or Doctoral students (in the target departments) are eligible.
- Excludes: Students from Mainland China, students in one‐year programmes, students on leave.
- Your application must go through your academic advisor/discussion with advisor.
- 2025 cycle the deadline was 19 September. (For “114學年度” meaning academic year 114 in Taiwan numbering
- For earlier 2020 example: Deadline 22 September.

##### **How To Apply**
- The application guidelines are sent to academic advisors by email (in the 2025 case: sent on 3 Sept)
- Students must discuss with their advisor and submit accordingly.
- The department will review applications and select awardees.

## Arrival Guide
### Things to do once you arrive at Taiwan
- Prepare 5,000~10,000 NTD cash initially.
- (Suggested) self-insurance.
- Fill (**D-3 before arrival**) Card and Taiwan 5000 [lucky draw](https://5000.taiwan.net.tw/index_en.html).
- Buy simcard (Suggestion: [Chunghwa Telecom](https://g.co/kgs/qSa8rRQ)).

### TPE Airport to NTUST with Trains

- Buy your EZCard at 711 (Suggested, purchase [30 days of Tpass](https://english.metro.taipei/cp.aspx?n=BECC2E7AC426F659&s=6A0905D6BC726B97))
- Top-up your EZCard
- Travel from [Toayuan Airport Terminal to Gongguan Station](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zdQu8uYPZRt5hooM9) - [**Schedule**](https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/en/_pages/travel-guide/timetable-search.php)

- Video by NTUST ISA: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebmUwua2Z2E

### Things to do after you arrive at NTUST
- Register at [NTUST (4F) Office of International Affairs (OIA)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WVmP2J1iDPt3rd2F6) to get a letter of acknowledgment
- Bring the letter from OIA to [National Immigration Agency (NIA)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PsbLFq2dhuJ55w7n6) to get your UI Number
- Go to Post Office Bank, either at [Gongguan](https://maps.app.goo.gl/H1cUn4rexdmh4d45A) or at [NTUST](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vH4uYReQZqmdfhsB6), to open a bank account. Please prepare:
  1.  UI Number
  2.  passport
  3.  mandarin name stamp
  4.  Phone number
- Check in to the dormitory, [dormitory 2](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ksxDTkLuqDTgNyXP6) for male and [dormitory 3](https://maps.app.goo.gl/eg74Pv16wXfRB7wV7) for female
- After you activate your number, you can also register at you bike to be able to use bike sharing service, [YouBike](https://www.youbike.com.tw/), which is available around Taipei City. YouBike Apps:
  - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tw.com.youbike.plus&hl=en&pli=1)
  - [Apple](https://apps.apple.com/tw/app/youbike%E5%BE%AE%E7%AC%91%E5%96%AE%E8%BB%8A-%E5%AE%98%E6%96%B9%E7%89%88/id1483423095)
- First [(Moshlem Friendly) Hotpot dinner](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BpzP1KZfCJWdqy5fA).
- You can buy your daily needs at the nearest supermarket, [Kuang Nan Wholeshale Shop](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KQAQMc2ESvPB7bW67)
